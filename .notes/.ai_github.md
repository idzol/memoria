## Release Notes Since Last Branch

### Added
- New story intro flow with `StoryCutscene` and `StoryChapterSequence`, including skippable chapter/biome transitions and fade-based handoff into `StoryLine`.
- In-battle victory overlay presentation for loot, with card and item rewards shown in-scene instead of hard-cutting away from combat.
- Center-screen tutorial overlays for StoryLine, WorldMap, Character Screen, and BattleScene that sit above the scene and wait for input to continue.
- Story biome progression support for replaying chapter sequences from the storyline flow.

### Changed
- Story navigation was refactored around `StoryLine` and `StoryMap`, replacing the older `StoryMapUI` / `MapSummary` naming and scene structure.
- World map generation and presentation moved further toward a hex-based biome layout, including fog-of-war, remembered-room summaries, impassable background nodes, and adjacency-driven travel.
- StoryMap was redesigned from a minimap card into a chapter-height remembered-room summary with room icons, titles, and remembered counts.
- Boss-clear flow now routes through chapter transitions so defeating a biome boss continues into the next chapter sequence before returning to the overworld.
- DataManager now backs off loading during sensitive scenes and pauses more aggressively around player activity to reduce hitches.

### Fixed
- BattleScene preview-card races, including the freed-node crash around `_fly_preview_card_to_discard`.
- World map boss-travel issues, including null-tree scroll timing and stale scene-path loading after the StoryLine rename.
- Tutorial/dialog layering issues so prompts and overlays render above scene objects.
- Main-menu duplicate-name UX now reliably returns focus to the hero name field.
- Character select cleanup removed the obsolete title header.
